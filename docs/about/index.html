About me and this site

This is Wu Jipeng’s personal site.

The Writeups section records the “fleeting customs” flourishing in the first half of the 21st century—the technologies I rely on for a living, such as computing systems, AI, and software engineering. There is occasional craftsmanship among them, and now and then a few humble philosophical reflections.

The Photographs section uses a camera to fix landscapes, people, and the customs of a particular time and place into albums.

The Works section collects art I make in my spare time—mainly games and AIGC pieces.

The Gears section catalogs my toys and productivity tools.
