  • E Ink Monitor

    E Ink displays are primarily aimed at people with dry eye, but they also suit people with neural hypersensitivity as a way to avoid visually induced migraines.

    Aug. 19, 2026 in Gears

    Since middle school, exposure to high-frequency flicker, patterns at certain spatial frequencies, or strong light has occasionally triggered severe migraines—deep orbital and radiating headaches accompanied by autonomic symptoms such as nausea and general fatigue.

    Over the years, ophthalmologists and neurologists could not offer an effective diagnosis or useful advice. I even found a rare disease that matched my symptoms closely, but my doctors did not accept it and simply called the problem visual fatigue. Other people’s visual fatigue is surely not this painful.
