Geometry of Representations

In the iterative loop of representation learning, one should try to estimate the intrinsic dimension of the embedded submanifold and design a reasonable ambient space at both the input and output ends.

Friday, August 14, 2026
Tags:
  • AI
  • Math

Consider an audio representation model that takes the logmel of an 8s clip as input. Its embedding pipeline is the following dimensionality-reduction process:

w∈R176400  →  Φ  (logmel)    x∈R11008  →  hθ  (ViT-L)    CLS∈R1024  →  gθ  (proj head)    z∈R256  →  ℓ2    z^∈S255 \begin{gathered} w\in\mathbb{R}^{176400} \;\xrightarrow{\;\Phi\;(\text{logmel})\;}\; x\in\mathbb{R}^{11008} \;\xrightarrow{\;h_\theta\;(\text{ViT-L})\;}\; \mathrm{CLS}\in\mathbb{R}^{1024} \\[6pt] \;\xrightarrow{\;g_\theta\;(\text{proj head})\;}\; z\in\mathbb{R}^{256} \;\xrightarrow{\;\ell_2\;}\; \hat z\in S^{255} \end{gathered} w∈R176400Φ(logmel)​x∈R11008hθ​(ViT-L)​CLS∈R1024gθ​(proj head)​z∈R256ℓ2​​z^∈S255​

  1. From the point-wise perspective, a single point www in the signal space is reparameterized into a point in a semantic metric space.

  2. From the ambient perspective, the dimension shrinks from 176400 in the signal space to 11008 in the pixel space, is mapped by ViT-L to 1024, then by the proj head to 256, and finally, after ℓ2\ell_2ℓ2​ normalization, onto the 255-dimensional sphere. The learnable part is fθ=ℓ2∘gθ∘hθf_\theta=\ell_2\circ g_\theta\circ h_\thetafθ​=ℓ2​∘gθ​∘hθ​, i.e. the segment R11008→S255\mathbb{R}^{11008}\to S^{255}R11008→S255; Φ\PhiΦ is a fixed, upstream compression.

  • By ambient we mean the support of the manifold; the most primitive ambient is the waveform signal space, i.e. R176400\mathbb{R}^{176400}R176400. After the logmel transform, it is mapped into the 11008-dimensional logmel pixel space. The final S255S^{255}S255 is likewise usually the ambient of the output submanifold, since the chosen output dimension is typically slightly higher than the intrinsic dimension.
  • Ambient spaces — the waveform signal space, the logmel pixel space, the spherical space of the final vectors — are not only directly visible but also human-prescribed. Keep in mind that their dimensions are non-natural and non-intrinsic.
  • Is it possible to make the ambient SD−1S^{D-1}SD−1 approximately coincide with the submanifold embedded in it? Difficult, but worth trying: on one hand, tune the hyperparameter DDD according to the estimated intrinsic dimension of the embedded submanifold; on the other hand, introduce SIGReg/VISReg regularization terms.

Min  =  supp⁡(μin)  ⊂  RP\mathcal{M}_{\text{in}}\;=\;\operatorname{supp}(\mu_{\text{in}})\;\subset\;\mathbb{R}^{P}Min​=supp(μin​)⊂RP

is, under the manifold hypothesis, an (approximately) smooth dind_{\text{in}}din​-dimensional embedded submanifold, with din≪Pd_{\text{in}}\ll Pdin​≪P.   1. From the manifold perspective,
Mproj  =  fθ(Min)  =  supp⁡((fθ)# μin)  ⊂  S255 \mathcal{M}_{\text{proj}} \;=\; f_\theta\big(\mathcal{M}_{\text{in}}\big) \;=\;\operatorname{supp}\big((f_\theta)_\#\,\mu_{\text{in}}\big)\;\subset\;S^{255} Mproj​=fθ​(Min​)=supp((fθ​)#​μin​)⊂S255
  • Every “manifold” discussed in this article is an embedded submanifold of the ambient at its respective level of abstraction, and its intrinsic dimension is the dimension of the tangent space.
  • Music JEPA’s training data is synthesized from NNN base audio clips via time-stretching, pitch-shifting, cropping, noise addition, and distortion. The logmel data is therefore the original content multiplied by the augmentation machinery, and the intrinsic dimension of the input manifold consists of a content dimension dCd_{\mathcal C}dC​ and an augmentation dimension dAd_{\mathcal A}dA​:
c∈C⏟content  ×  α∈A⏟augmentation  →  Ψ    x∈R11008 \underbrace{c\in\mathcal C}_{\text{content}} \;\times\; \underbrace{\alpha\in A}_{\text{augmentation}} \;\xrightarrow{\;\Psi\;}\; x \in \mathbb{R}^{11008} contentc∈C​​×augmentationα∈A​​Ψ​x∈R11008

  • Reducing the intrinsic dimension of the embedded submanifold gets closer to the essence of dimensionality reduction in representation learning — learning invariance between the anchor and its degraded versions: from the input manifold’s din≈dC+dAd_{\text{in}}\approx d_{\mathcal C}+d_Adin​≈dC​+dA​ down to the output manifold’s dproj≈dCd_{\text{proj}}\approx d_{\mathcal C}dproj​≈dC​. The model should respect only the content in retrieval tasks while ignoring the augmentations (in this scenario, augmentation is degradation).

  • How does one estimate the intrinsic dimension of the embedded submanifold? There are well-established intrinsic dimension estimation methods, such as TwoNN and the Levina–Bickel MLE. The number of points within a neighborhood of radius rrr grows as ∼rd\sim r^{d}∼rd; TwoNN / MLE simply invert the nearest-neighbor distance ratios to recover this ddd.
