This is **Wu Jipeng**'s personal site.

The [**Writeups**](/writeups/) section records the “fleeting customs” flourishing in the first half of the 21st century—the technologies I rely on for a living, such as computing systems, AI, and software engineering. There is occasional craftsmanship among them, and now and then a few humble philosophical reflections.

The [**Photographs**](/photos/) section uses a camera to fix landscapes, people, and the customs of a particular time and place into albums.

The [**Works**](/works/) section collects art I make in my spare time—mainly games and AIGC pieces.

The [**Gears**](/gears/) section catalogs my toys and productivity tools.
